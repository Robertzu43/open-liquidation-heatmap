## How it works

- **Optional participation.** Donate without requesting recognition and you stay off this board. Use a nickname if you prefer.
- **Manual confirmation.** Open a supporter request with your preferred public credit and transaction details. Requests are public. A maintainer confirms the payment and attribution using the process below before adding anyone; there is no automatic wallet tracking or instant ranking.
- **Cumulative ranking.** Rank participating supporters by the total USDT actually received at the project's Tron address, excluding network fees. Count each transaction once. Equal totals share a rank and title; the next rank follows competition ranking (1, 1, 3).
- **Consent.** Listed supporters agree to publication of their chosen name and cumulative amount. A transaction link alone does not prove who sent a payment. Uncertain or disputed claims remain unlisted; wallet ownership is never inferred from public chain activity.
- **A thank-you.** Recognition does not buy trading advice, paid features, guaranteed feature priority, or financial returns. It celebrates support for continued development.

You can ask to change or remove your current entry through an issue. As with other public GitHub content, earlier revisions may remain in repository history.

## Confirming a shoutout

For a verified listing, the maintainer replies to your request with a unique, readable message containing the repository name, issue URL, transaction ID, and a one-time challenge. Sign that **plain message** with the wallet that sent the USDT and post the signature in the request. The maintainer verifies the signature against the sending address and checks the USDT receipt before adding your chosen credit.

This is a message signature, not a transaction or token approval. No private key, seed phrase, login, or account screenshot is needed. Donations sent from an exchange account may not support this proof and can remain unlisted. If recognition matters to you, check that your sending wallet supports Tron message signing before donating. Anonymous support is always welcome.
