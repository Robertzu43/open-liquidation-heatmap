# Colombia build ideas

Notes only. This file is not part of the liquidation-heatmap product.

Searched on Tuesday 27 Oct 2026, about 12:15 p.m. America/Bogota. A few targeted web searches, then this note. Under three minutes of searching. Snippets and result pages, not a click-through of every ranking URL and not a new Google Trends pull.

Google Trends window already used for the volumes below: geo Colombia, weeks of 26 Oct 2025 through 25 Oct 2026. Volumes and the CPC / CCCE / Keyword Planner figures are that earlier research. This pass only checks who already has a page.

## How each idea is marked

- **Official site exists.** The government (or the insurer, for SOAT) already does the trámite.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** News sites, calculators, or guides showed up in search on 27 Oct 2026.
- **Gap still open.** What is still worth building after those two facts. A clearer page that links to the official site can still be worth it. An empty market is a different claim, and these searches do not support that claim.

The opportunity on the free list is a plain explainer that answers one question and links to the official site. It is not a second SIMIT, RUNT, DIAN, SISBÉN, ADRES, Colpensiones, SECOP, or SGC.

## Free help

Search volumes are monthly estimates from the earlier research.

### 1. DIAN 2026 calendar — do I file, which date, minimum penalty, link to file

About 2.7M searches/month.

- **Official site exists.** DIAN publishes the 2026 tax calendar PDF, a June 2026 press note on who must file renta (about 7 million people, 12 Aug–26 Oct 2026 by the last two NIT digits), and monthly “vencimientos” pages. Filing stays on DIAN.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** Bloomberg Línea has the 2026 renta date table. Toke already has a renta page with the thresholds, a minimum-sanction figure for 2026, and a WhatsApp reminder. Accounting blogs are cited from that page.
- **Gap still open.** Narrow. One mobile page that does four things in order: do I have to file, which date is mine, what the minimum late sanction is this year, and a button to the official DIAN filing entry. Label it as a guide. Toke and Bloomberg already cover pieces of this.

### 2. SIMIT guide — plate or cédula, then the official consulta

About 2.2M.

- **Official site exists.** The consulta is the Federación Colombiana de Municipios SIMIT (news and guides send people to `fcm.org.co/simit`).
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** El Tiempo, Noticias Caracol, El País, and Comparatodo already publish the steps. A non-government domain, `simitconsultas.com`, also appeared in search with a lookup box. Treat lookalike domains as a reason to warn people, not as a model to copy.
- **Gap still open.** A short guide whose only action is the official SIMIT link, plus the plate-versus-cédula difference and a warning about pages that ask for the document number. Another consulta form is not the product.

### 3. RUNT por placa

About 1.8M.

- **Official site exists.** RUNT consulta por placa on `runt.gov.co` / the public portal. The full vehicle record asks for the plate and the owner’s document. SOAT or RTM checks are separate official paths.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** VehiculoAlDia has a long “RUNT por placa 2026” guide (updated May 2026) that already points at the public portal and explains the captcha and the “origen” field.
- **Gap still open.** Narrow. Same trámites site as DIAN and SIMIT, one page, official link, no cloned form.

### 4. SISBÉN / RUI score explainer + Ventanilla Social

About 1.2M. “Puntaje RUI” was rising in the earlier Trends window.

- **Official site exists.** Ventanilla Social (`ventanillasocial.dnp.gov.co`) is the DNP place to see the RUI classification and download the certificate. From 1 Aug 2026 the RUI is the new targeting register. SISBÉN IV groups still show during the transition.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** El País and Noticias Caracol already explain Consulta RUI and groups A–D. A non-official domain, `sdp.sisben-gov.co`, also ranked. It is not a `gov.co` site.
- **Gap still open.** A plain page that says the result is a group (A1–D21), not the old 0–100 score, and that a group is not by itself a payment. Warn about lookalike domains. News already does most of this.

### 5. ADRES steps to see which EPS

About 1.2M.

- **Official site exists.** `adres.gov.co/consulte-su-eps` and the BDUA afiliado page. The result is whatever the EPS reported. ADRES says it does not issue affiliation certificates.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** Less than SIMIT. City pages (Tocancipá, Sopó) mostly repeat the official link.
- **Gap still open.** A five-step page is still reasonable and less crowded than DIAN or SIMIT. The consulta itself is already free on ADRES. Say clearly when the screen can be wrong (transfer, inactive EPS, special regimes that this consulta does not show).

### 6. Colpensiones certificate for declaración de renta

About 673k.

- **Official site exists.** Colpensiones Sede Electrónica, “Certificado de Deducidos y Devengados,” for pensioners. Colpensiones also has its own short article on downloading it.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** Caracol Radio (4 Sep 2026), El País, and Minuto60 already publish the 2026 steps (year gravable 2025, 1 Jan–31 Dec 2025).
- **Gap still open.** Seasonal. A permanent URL can outlast a September news article, aimed at pensioners who need that one PDF before their DIAN date. The download stays on Colpensiones.

### 7. Salario mínimo and retención calculator for 2026

“Calculadora” about 1.5M. Context: minimum wage up about 23.7%.

- **Official site exists.** DIAN sets the UVT. Several calculators attribute UVT 2026 of $52,374 to DIAN Resolución 000238 of 15 Dec 2025. The wage figure is a decree, not a calculator.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** Gestiova, Toolboxday, Tuliqui, CalculaFaro, and Gerencie all have 2026 salary withholding calculators. Toolboxday shows a 2026 minimum wage of $1,750,905 on its calculator. Gerencie goes further: procedure 1 and procedure 2, and a saved history per worker.
- **Gap still open.** A free public calculator is crowded. See the paid-calculator note in the money list. If a free page is built, show the UVT year, the wage year, and the article 383 table, and point at the decree and the DIAN resolution.

### 8. Temblor hoy, plus how to turn on the phone earthquake alarm

- **Official site exists.** Servicio Geológico Colombiano: `sgc.gov.co`, the sismos visor at `sgc.gov.co/sismos`, and live traces at `trazas.sgc.gov.co`. This search did not confirm a quake reading for 27 Oct 2026. Some snippets of the visor still showed early September events, so treat “what happened today” as a link to SGC, not as a number copied from search.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** El Colombiano and Noticias RCN (10 Aug 2026) already explain Android’s earthquake alerts and say iPhone does not have that native alert for Colombia. They name third-party apps (Sismo Detector and others).
- **Gap still open.** Thin. One page with two links: today’s SGC list, and the Android settings path (Seguridad y emergencia → Alertas de terremotos, or Ubicación → Avanzada). Skip a homemade seismic network.

### 9. Monthly Bogotá theft map by barrio

Datos Abiertos Bogotá, updated 22 Oct 2026, localidad counts through Sep 2026. Police SIEDCO is monthly, about day 16 after the month, not a daily feed. Earlier research: “hurto” search demand is near zero next to DIAN.

- **Official site exists.** Dataset “Delito de Alto Impacto. Bogotá D.C.” on Datos Abiertos Bogotá, metadata updated 22 Oct 2026, with shapefile, GeoJSON, and a Mapas Bogotá view. The Secretaría Distrital de Seguridad also publishes a public SIEDCO map service.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** CrimeAnalizer is a 2018 student project listed on datos.gov.co. This search did not find a current, popular consumer “hurto por barrio” product.
- **Gap still open.** A readable monthly chart by localidad or barrio, updated when the open file updates, with the lag stated in the title (“through September, published 22 Oct”). Low search demand, so it is not in the first build. Publish counts for a barrio or localidad. Leave exact addresses and point-level victim locations off the site. Use the open dataset the city already publishes.

### 10. SECOP II guide for a small contractor

About 1.5M.

- **Official site exists.** Colombia Compra Eficiente already has the persona-natural FAQ, the proveedor registration steps on `operaciones.colombiacompra.gov.co`, and registration PDFs.
- **Private explainer sites already rank.** This short search mostly returned official pages, not a dominant independent guide.
- **Gap still open.** Better than the theft map, weaker than DIAN. A checklist for one persona natural (user, then proveedor account, which PDFs, NIT without the check digit) can be shorter than the official manual. Registration stays on SECOP II.

## Extra money

### 1. Ads on the free trámite pages

Earlier note: DIAN CPC about $0.83.

- **Who already does it.** The explainer sites above already compete for these queries, which is why a CPC exists.
- **What is still worth building.** Ads on a page you own, after the page is useful and sends people to the official site. The CPC is a price for crowded queries, not proof of an empty page.

### 2. WhatsApp reminder subscription, about $36,000–$72,000 COP/month (DIAN, SIMIT, SOAT dates)

- **Who already does it.** Toke sells a WhatsApp renta reminder (7-day trial, updated 11 Sep 2026) and already states a 2026 minimum sanction. Pymento is a DIAN calendar for accountants with email reminders on the free plan and WhatsApp on a Pro plan at $19,900 COP/month. DIAN’s own WhatsApp (public post, 12 Oct 2026) is for overdue balances and the official payment receipt, not a paid personal calendar. Bancautos already offers a free SOAT / tecnomecánica reminder.
- **What is still worth building.** A single consumer reminder that covers one person’s renta date, a SIMIT check, and a SOAT date. The category is occupied, and $36,000–$72,000 sits above the Pymento price that already showed up. Test a lower price before building the $36k–$72k plan as the offer.

### 3. Paid saved calculator for accountants and freelancers (UVT / wage decree)

- **Who already does it.** Gerencie’s 2026 calculator stores each worker, applies the annual caps, and does procedure 1 and procedure 2. The free calculators in item 7 cover one-off math.
- **What is still worth building.** Only a sharper niche than Gerencie (for example a saved file that follows the wage decree and the UVT resolution by name, exportable for one accountant’s clients). A generic “save my calculation” product is already on the market.

### 4. Monthly WhatsApp catalog for a tienda or restaurant

Earlier research: almost nobody searches “página web.” CCCE 2026: 2.3% of micronegocios have a website, 48.5% use messaging.

- **Who already does it.** WhatsApp Business already has a catalog. Freelancers already set these up. This search was not aimed at agencies, and it did not need to be: the demand is local sales, not a Google query for a new kind of site.
- **What is still worth building.** A done-for-you setup sold in one city, priced as a service. A new website product fights a channel people already use.

### 5. Canva template pack (menú, factura, cotización, estado de WhatsApp)

Earlier research: Canva about 4.1M searches, CPC about $1.31.

- **Who already does it.** Canva’s own Colombia site already has free editable templates for menús, facturas, cotizaciones, and listas de precios. A Gumroad kit at $6 (control de negocio, menú, cotización, not a Canva file and not a factura) also exists.
- **What is still worth building.** A small pack of WhatsApp-status images plus a factura layout that matches Colombian fields (NIT, resolución de facturación). People searching “Canva” are already on Canva, where the core templates are free. Treat this as a cheap side file, not as the company.

### 6. SOAT reminder / comparison, November–January, lead fee

Earlier research: SOAT peaks in late December.

- **Who already does it.** R5, Seguros Falabella, Previsora, and Davibank already quote and sell SOAT by plate. Falabella’s page says the price is set by the Superintendencia Financiera and should match across sellers for the same vehicle category. Bancautos already explains the RUNT “fecha fin de vigencia” and offers a free reminder.
- **What is still worth building.** A November–January reminder that hands the person to an insurer, if a lead fee is actually offered. A comparison table of regulated prices is a weak product, because the tariff is the same. Confirm the lead fee with one insurer before building the page.

### 7. One-time website for one accountant or lawyer in one city (about $1.8–$4 million COP)

Earlier Keyword Planner note, Sep 2025–Aug 2026: “contador en Bogotá” about 390 searches/month.

- **Who already does it.** Ordinary freelance web work. The query volume is small next to the trámite pages.
- **What is still worth building.** One paid site for one named professional, as a services job. It is not a scalable page and it is not an empty market.

## Suggested build order

Search volume still puts the first three trámites first. Build them as guides that link out.

1. **DIAN 2026 calendar.** Highest volume. One screen: must I file, my date, minimum sanction, official DIAN link. Compete with Bloomberg and Toke on clarity, not on a second tax portal.
2. **SIMIT.** Second. Official link plus the plate-versus-cédula note and a warning about lookalike consultas.
3. **RUNT por placa.** Third. Same site, same pattern. VehiculoAlDia already has the long version, so keep this page short.
4. Then **ADRES** (less crowded) and **SECOP II persona natural** (official docs are long).
5. **Colpensiones certificate** only as a permanent URL tied to the DIAN calendar, for pensioners.
6. Skip a new free retención calculator unless it cites the decree and the UVT resolution more clearly than Gerencie and Gestiova. Skip the theft map until the first three pages have traffic. Skip a homemade earthquake feed. Link SGC.

Ship the money ideas only after a free page has visits. The reminder price to test is closer to products already on the market than to $36,000–$72,000. The Canva pack and the single professional website are services, not the first product.

## Boundaries

- Send people to the official site for the actual consulta, filing, payment, or download.
- Leave cédula and plate collection off any page that is not the official portal.
- Theft figures stay at barrio or localidad. No exact addresses, no point map of homes.
- Explain dates, thresholds, and sanctions from the published decree, the DIAN resolution, and the official calendar. This project does not help anyone under-report income or skip a filing.
- These pages are guides. They are not DIAN, SIMIT, RUNT, DNP, ADRES, Colpensiones, Colombia Compra, or the SGC. Say that on each page.
